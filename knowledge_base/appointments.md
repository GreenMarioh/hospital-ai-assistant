# Appointments

## Booking
You can book, reschedule or cancel appointments in the patient portal, by asking the booking assistant, or at the front desk. Appointments are usually 30 minutes long.

## Cancelling and rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule as early as possible so the slot can be offered to someone on the waitlist. You can cancel any time before the appointment starts.

## Waitlist
If your preferred day is full, join the waitlist for that doctor and date. If a slot opens you will be notified by email or SMS and can book it in the portal.

## Reminders
Reminders are sent by email and SMS 24 hours and 2 hours before your appointment.

## What to bring
Bring photo identification, your insurance card if you have one, a list of current medications, and any recent test reports.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled.
