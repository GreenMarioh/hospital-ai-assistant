# Hospital Policies

## Patient privacy
Your health information is confidential. Only staff involved in your care can access your records, and every access is logged. You can view your own records in the portal.

## Medical records requests
Copies of records are released to the patient on written request with photo identification. Requests from other people need the patient's written consent.

## Photography and phones
Photography of other patients or staff is not permitted. Please keep phones on silent in wards.

## Smoking
The hospital campus is smoke-free.

## Valuables
Please leave valuables at home. The hospital is not responsible for lost personal items.
