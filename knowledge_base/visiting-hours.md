# Visiting Hours

Demo hospital knowledge base. Replace with your hospital's real policies.

## General wards
Visiting hours are 10:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 20:00 every day. A maximum of two visitors per patient at a time is allowed.

## Intensive care unit (ICU)
ICU visits are limited to 15 minutes, twice a day (11:00 and 18:00), for immediate family only. Visitors must wash their hands and follow staff instructions.

## Children visiting
Children under 12 may visit only with an adult and only in general wards, not in the ICU or isolation rooms.

## Maternity ward
The partner or one nominated support person may stay with the patient at all hours. Other visitors follow the general ward hours.
