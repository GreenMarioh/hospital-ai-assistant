# Emergency, Location and Parking

## Emergencies
If you think you or someone else is having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or come to the Emergency department immediately. The portal chat and appointments are not for emergencies.

## Location
The main entrance is on the ground floor of the central building. The Emergency entrance is separate and signposted.

## Parking
Visitor parking is available in the east car park. The first 30 minutes are free. Accessible bays are next to the main entrance.

## Contact
The front desk is open every day from 08:00 to 20:00.
