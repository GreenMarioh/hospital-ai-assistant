# Departments and Services

## Cardiology
Heart and vascular care, including heart rhythm checks, blood pressure management and follow-up after cardiac events. Outpatient clinics run Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.

## General Medicine
Primary and internal medicine for adults. This is the best starting point if you are unsure which department you need.

## Orthopedics
Bones, joints and muscles, including fractures, sports injuries and joint pain. Fracture clinic runs on weekday mornings.

## Pediatrics
Care for infants, children and teenagers up to age 17, including routine check-ups and vaccinations.

## Dermatology
Skin, hair and nail conditions. Appointments are by booking only.

## Neurology
Conditions of the brain and nervous system, such as recurring headaches, numbness or seizure follow-up.

## Emergency
The Emergency department is open 24 hours a day, every day, and does not need an appointment.
